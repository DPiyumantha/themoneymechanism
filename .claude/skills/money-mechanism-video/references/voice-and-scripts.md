# Voice, vibe and scripts

## The vibe

**Premium, confident, mechanical.** Think a sharp friend who works in finance explaining something over coffee: calm, a little wry, never shouting. The page feels like opening the back of a watch and seeing the gears turn.

- We explain, we don't preach. No "you're doing money wrong."
- We admire clever business models even when we expose them.
- No hype, no get-rich-quick, no crypto shilling, no "passive income" promises.
- No fake luxury imagery (Lamborghinis, cash stacks, private jets).

## Sound American without saying "USA"

The audience is American. Never mention that. Let the details do it:

- American spelling (color, analyze, center), $ amounts, miles, °F, "Nov 7".
- US touchpoints as shared knowledge: Costco, Target, Walmart, Starbucks, Amazon, the Fed, Wall Street, the IRS, 401(k), credit scores, Black Friday, Super Bowl ads, tipping.
- Never "for American viewers", "in the USA", "America's…" in a hook.

## The 5-beat structure (every video)

| Beat | Time | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Hook | 0–3 s | A surprising claim, mid-thought. Stops the scroll. |
| 2. Setup | 3–12 s | What everyone assumes. |
| 3. Mechanism | 12–45 s | How the money really moves. 2–4 steps, one visual each. |
| 4. Payoff | last 5 s | One sentence that reframes everything. |
| 5. End card | 3.5 s | Automatic. |

Spoken pace: about 2.6 words per second. A 45 s video ≈ 115 words.

## Hooks that work

Under 8 words on screen. One highlighted word (the surprise).

- **The contradiction:** "Costco sells this at a loss." / "Same price. 40 years."
- **The hidden number:** "Starbucks is secretly a bank." / "Your $5 latte costs 40¢."
- **The question they never asked:** "Why is it always $9.99?"
- **The reversal:** "Netflix wants you to share passwords." (only if true)
- **The scale:** "This app makes $1M an hour."

Never: "Wait for it", "You won't believe", "Watch till the end", "Guys", ALL CAPS.

## Topic bank (starting points; verify every fact)

- Why gas station prices end in 9/10 of a cent
- How Costco makes most of its profit from memberships
- Why airlines make more from credit cards than seats
- Why movie theaters really make money on popcorn
- How "free shipping over $35" changes what you buy
- Why grocery stores put milk at the back
- How Starbucks gift cards work like a bank
- Why Apple sells cases separately
- How "buy now, pay later" makes money
- Why hotel prices change by the hour

## On-screen text rules

- Sentence case. One idea per screen. Hooks under 8 words; labels under 10.
- Numbers as figures: "$1.50", "40 years", "3x", "$4.2B".
- On-screen text is not the voice-over. It's the headline of what's being said.

## Post caption (give this to the user with every video)

```
<Hook as a question or statement, under 12 words>

<2 sentences: the mechanism in plain words>

<A question that invites comments: "What price trick gets you every time?">

Sources: <short list>
#TheMoneyMechanism #<Topic> #<Company or concept>
```

2–3 hashtags only. No "follow for more" in the caption (the end card does that).
