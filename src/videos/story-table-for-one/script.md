## Facts and sources (verified 2026-11-09)

- **Origin:** Singles' Day started as an informal "joke" holiday on 11/11 (Bachelors' Day, commonly traced to Nanjing University students in the 1990s; that story is widely repeated but unverified, so the script only says "a joke holiday in China").
- **2009:** Alibaba (Taobao) ran the first 11/11 online sale. Fortune (Nov 11, 2019): $7.8M GMV in the first year. https://fortune.com/2019/11/11/china-singles-day-sales-alibaba/
- **2021 total:** Alibaba reported 540.3 billion yuan (about $84.54 billion) GMV over the 11-day 2021 event (Reuters, via Business Today / Euronews). https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/world/story/alibaba-records-8454-bn-in-orders-in-singles-day-311991-2021-11-12 ; https://www.euronews.com/next/2021/11/12/us-singles-day-alibaba . Note the window was 11 days, not one day; the voice-over says "reported" and avoids "in one day".
- **Black Friday + Cyber Monday 2021 (US online):** $8.9B + $10.7B = $19.6B, Adobe Analytics. https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2021/11/30/adobe-consumers-spent-10-8-billion-on-cyber-monday-109-8-billion-so-far-this-holiday-season
- **2022:** Alibaba (and JD) did not disclose a Singles' Day GMV total for the first time since 2009; Alibaba said results were in line with 2021. https://www.euronews.com/2022/11/11/alibaba-singles-day ; https://technode.com/2022/11/14/singles-day-2022-alibaba-and-jd-keep-quiet-on-gmv-for-the-first-time/ ; SCMP https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3199393/e-commerce-giants-alibaba-jdcom-keep-singles-day-sales-results-under-wraps-amid-chinas-economic-woes
- 2025: Alibaba again shared no official total (third-party estimates differ, e.g. Syntun about 1.70 trillion yuan market-wide); not used in the video.
- No Alibaba/Taobao logos or app UI; calendar, bags and cup are generic.
