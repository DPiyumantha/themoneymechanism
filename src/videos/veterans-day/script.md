## Post caption
Your bank is closed today. The stores aren't.

Veterans Day is a federal holiday, so the Fed and most banks close and the bond market takes the day off, while the stock market and most stores stay open. If you're sending a transfer today, it may not land until tomorrow.

Thank a veteran, and check your transfer date. Anything you're waiting on to clear?
