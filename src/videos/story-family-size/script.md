# Caught in the Machine · Ep 3 — Family Size

Slug `story-family-size` · Composition `MM-StoryFamilySize` · Reels 1080×1920, 30 fps · about 45 s + end card
Story series tab, Ep 3 (replaces Calendar #8, "Shrinkflation"), Tue Nov 10, slot B.

**Money concept:** shrinkflation. Smaller package, same price. Companies shrink the package instead of raising the price because shoppers watch prices, not ounces. The tool that catches it is the unit price (price per ounce) on the shelf tag.
**Cast:** Maya (Sulafat), Jake (Puck). Voice-over: `public/videos/story-family-size/voiceover.mp3` (Gemini TTS, gemini-3.8-flash-tts, 44.9 s).

## Dialogue

```
Jake: Movie night! I got the family size.
Maya: For one person.
Jake: It's a big family of one. Wait. Why is it half air?
Maya: Check the weight on the front.
Jake: It says nine point two five ounces. It used to be nine point seven five.
Maya: Same price, though?
Jake: Same price!

Maya: It's called shrinkflation. When costs go up, companies can raise the price, or quietly shrink the package. Shrinking usually wins, because we watch prices, not ounces.
Jake: So how do I catch it?
Maya: The tiny number on the shelf tag. Price per ounce. That's the real price.

Jake: Got it. Can I have some of yours?
Maya: Mine shrank too.
```

## Facts and sources (verified 2026-11-09)

- Frito-Lay confirmed it cut a regular bag of Doritos from 9.75 oz to 9.25 oz (about five fewer chips) with the price unchanged; the company said it "took just a little bit out of the bag" to keep the same price. Newsweek, "Not Your Imagination: Doritos Bag Has 5 Fewer Chips Due To 'Shrinkflation'" (2022): https://www.newsweek.com/not-your-imagination-doritos-bag-has-5-fewer-chips-due-shrinkflation-1689450
- Yale economics paper dating the downsizing to 2021: L. Allou, "Shrinkflation in the Snack Aisle: Evidence from Doritos' 2021 Downsizing" (2025): https://economics.yale.edu/sites/default/files/2025-05/LILA%20ALLOU_%20Shrinkflation%20in%20the%20Snack%20Aisle_%20Evidence%20from%20Doritos%E2%80%99%202021%20Downsizing.pdf
- Definition and "shrink instead of raise the price": St. Louis Fed, "Beyond Inflation Numbers: Shrinkflation and Skimpflation" (Dec 2022): https://www.stlouisfed.org/publications/page-one-economics/2022/12/01/beyond-inflation-numbers-shrinkflation-and-skimpflation
- Caveat: the documented cut was to the regular bag, not a "family size" bag. The on-screen source line says "one popular chip bag", and the family-size joke is Jake's, not a claim.
- Unit pricing is shown on most US shelf tags as price per ounce (or per unit). Check the real shelf tag wording before posting if you add a claim about it.
