## Script
Jake: Movie night! I got the family size.
Maya: For one person.
Jake: It's a big family of one. Wait. Why is it half air?
Maya: Check the weight on the front.
Jake: It says nine point two five ounces. It used to be nine point seven five.
Maya: Same price, though?
Jake: Same price!

Maya: It's called shrinkflation. When costs go up, companies can raise the price, or quietly shrink the package. Shrinking usually wins, because we watch prices, not ounces.
Jake: So how do I catch it?
Maya: The tiny number on the shelf tag. Price per ounce. That's the real price.

Jake: Got it. Can I have some of yours?
Maya: Mine shrank too.
